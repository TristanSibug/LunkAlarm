Native LunkAlarm for Android 8.0 and later, built with Kotlin and Jetpack Compose.

- Large clock, swipe navigation, compact saved alarms and active countdowns.
- Optional names, weekday routines, editing, exact duplicates, and swipe-to-delete with haptic feedback.
- Floating Add alarm / Save changes buttons and native looping time/test controls.
- The same fourteen themes and ten offline ringtones, with previews and audio-file imports.
- Android exact alarms and foreground alarm playback, lock-screen presentation when allowed, and recovery after restarting the phone.
- Timed typing/math wake-up tests, automatic math submission, answering sound switch, volume slider and gradual fade.

Download **LunkAlarm-Android-1.0.0.apk**, open it on your phone, and allow installation from your browser or file manager when Android asks. In LunkAlarm, allow **Alarms & reminders**, **Notifications**, and **Full-screen alarms** for lock-screen presentation. Overall sound level uses your device's **alarm volume**.

This is a signed development APK for personal testing, not a Google Play release. Test a short alarm with the phone locked before relying on it overnight. Android's Force stop disables app alarms until the app is reopened; Android and device battery settings control background behavior.
