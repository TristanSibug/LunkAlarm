# LunkAlarm 2.0 — App Icons and Refreshed Themes

Choose how LunkAlarm looks in the app and on your Home Screen. Swipe down from the big clock to open About, then use **Theme** for the app’s colors or **App icon** to preview and pick one of 18 bell icons. The two settings are independent on Android and iOS.

### What's changed

- **Cobalt & Coral** now uses pale blue alarm cards with navy text and coral repeat-day markers.
- **Heritage** uses its oxblood red for text and interface icons.
- **Midnight Cherry** uses blue for alarm names and enabled switches.
- **iOS** saves the alarm time as one selection when the minute wheel wraps backward from 00 to 59.

### Downloads

- **Android** · Install the attached `LunkAlarm-Android-2.0.apk` over the permanently signed 1.10.4 APK to keep your alarms, timers, and settings.
- **iOS** · Sign and install the attached `LunkAlarm-iOS-2.0-unsigned.ipa` using your usual sideloading method.
